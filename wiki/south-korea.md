# South Korea

## Fast fashion and textile waste

- South Koreans spent **US$58 billion** on fashion products between March
  2024 and February 2025
  [Korea fast fashion boom](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md).
- Korea discards an estimated **800,000 tonnes of clothing and textiles
  annually**, and recycles only **12%** of it locally
  [Korea fast fashion boom](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md).
- **80% of clothing-waste management is handled by the private sector**, not
  a government program — the opposite of Korea's food waste system (below)
  [Korea fast fashion boom](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md).
- Exported used clothing goes primarily to **India, Malaysia, and Pakistan**,
  and Korea's domestic second-hand market is expected to reach **US$30
  billion** this year, having doubled since 2021
  [Korea fast fashion boom](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md).
- Wear Again Lab founder Juyeon Jung: **"The system we currently have is a
  problem... [it is] not regulated by the government,"** and **"We have to
  deal with our own waste"**
  [Korea fast fashion boom](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md).

## Contrast: Korea's food waste system

Korea's textile situation is the mirror image of how it handles food waste.
Korea **recycles 95% of its food waste** under a mandatory, weight-based fee
system: residents pay by the gram, enforced nationally since 2013, after a
staged rollout that began with a 1995 disposal fee and a 2005 landfill ban
[Korea food waste system](../sources/korea-food-waste-system.md). That
system cost the average household **about $6 a month**
[Korea food waste system](../sources/korea-food-waste-system.md). Textiles
have no equivalent: no fee, no mandatory collection, no government
infrastructure — just private exporters
[Korea fast fashion boom](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md).

## ⚠️ Note on figures

The food-waste recycling rate (95%) is from a 2022 article; if the team needs
a current number, that source flags it should be checked for a newer reading
[Korea food waste system](../sources/korea-food-waste-system.md). The
textile-waste figures (800,000 tonnes/year, 12% recycled) are from a single
2025-dated article and haven't been cross-checked against a second source.
