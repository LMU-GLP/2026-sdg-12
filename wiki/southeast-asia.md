# Southeast Asia

Southeast Asia is where much of the world's discarded and secondhand clothing
ends up, and where several small circular-fashion programs are already
running.

## The problem: secondhand clothing imports

- **Indonesia has banned secondhand clothing imports since 2015**, to protect
  its domestic textile industry
  [Indonesia secondhand clothing crackdown](../sources/indonesia-secondhand-clothing-crackdown.md).
- In a single March 2023 raid, Indonesian officials destroyed **more than
  7,000 bundles** of illegally imported used clothing in West Java, valued
  at **$5.3 billion**
  [Indonesia secondhand clothing crackdown](../sources/indonesia-secondhand-clothing-crackdown.md).
- Trade Minister Zulkifli Hasan on the enforcement: **"This is a follow-up to
  a directive from the President. We have done this several times and this
  is the climax."**
  [Indonesia secondhand clothing crackdown](../sources/indonesia-secondhand-clothing-crackdown.md)
- The ban is contested by the people it affects most: seller Siti said,
  **"If there are no clothes, that means no money,"** and a business law
  lecturer, Kosman Samosir, argued the trade **"gives people who don't have
  the means to buy branded clothes at full price the chance to still own a
  piece"**
  [Indonesia secondhand clothing crackdown](../sources/indonesia-secondhand-clothing-crackdown.md).
- This source documents enforcement of the import ban, not the share of
  imports that becomes landfill waste versus resold stock — that figure is a
  gap in this wiki, not something either source states
  [Indonesia secondhand clothing crackdown](../sources/indonesia-secondhand-clothing-crackdown.md).

## Existing solutions in the region

- **Kloth Cares (Malaysia)**, founded 2016, estimates Malaysians produce
  **almost 2,000 tonnes of textile waste a day** and had collected **over
  18,000 kg of fabric by 2017**, with a 2019 goal of **188,888 kg**
  [Kloth Cares / circular fashion](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md).
- **Style Theory (Singapore)** rents clothing for single events instead of
  selling it
  [Kloth Cares / circular fashion](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md).
- **moreloop (Thailand)** connects factories with leftover "deadstock" fabric
  to buyers, redirecting material that would otherwise be waste
  [Kloth Cares / circular fashion](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md).
- Fashion Revolution has coordinators in **nine Southeast Asian countries**,
  but its own Malaysian coordinator, Sasibai Kimis, says public awareness of
  ethical-fashion issues **"is still in its infancy in Malaysia"**
  [Kloth Cares / circular fashion](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md).
