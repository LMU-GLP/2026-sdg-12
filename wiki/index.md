# Wiki index

Catalog of every page in this wiki. **The active research track is fast
fashion in Southeast Asia** — start with
[SDG 12 targets applied to fast fashion](fast-fashion-sdg12.md), then
[global scale](fast-fashion-global-scale.md), then the
[Southeast Asia](southeast-asia-fast-fashion.md) and
[solutions](fast-fashion-solutions.md) pages.

The food-waste pages below are earlier research the team is no longer
pursuing. They're kept as-is (per CLAUDE.md: source files are never edited
to fit an argument, and pages aren't deleted just because direction
changed) but nothing new is being built on them.

## Active track: fast fashion (Southeast Asia)

| Page | Summary |
|---|---|
| [SDG 12 targets applied to fast fashion](fast-fashion-sdg12.md) | Targets 12.3, 12.5, and 12.8 read against clothing instead of food, and where each other fast-fashion page fits against them. |
| [Global scale of fast fashion waste](fast-fashion-global-scale.md) | ~92 million tonnes of textile waste generated globally per year, projected to reach 134 million tonnes by 2030 — with no Southeast Asia-specific figure in this dataset. |
| [Southeast Asia: fast fashion (Indonesia)](southeast-asia-fast-fashion.md) | Indonesia's secondhand-clothing import ban and 2023 enforcement crackdown, set against the household demand it cuts off — plus a flagged, uncorroborated claim that Indonesia ranks second globally in textile waste. |
| [Fast fashion solutions: CASHEW](fast-fashion-solutions.md) | A 2023 Indonesian youth education program whose own survey found most of its audience didn't know what fast fashion or fashion waste was — an awareness-gap intervention, not a policy or market one. |
