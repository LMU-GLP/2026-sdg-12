# Existing programs: CASHEW (Cut Fast Fashion and Waste)

Source: [CASHEW program](../sources/cashew-indonesia-fast-fashion-program.md)

Where [Too Good To Go](consumer-solutions.md) (from the earlier food-waste
track) is a for-profit product intervening at a purchase decision, CASHEW is
a nonprofit, youth-led **education** intervention — the fast-fashion track's
version of a Target 12.8 (awareness) solution rather than a Target 12.3/12.5
(waste/consumption) one.

## What it is

CASHEW launched **June 18, 2023**, run by CIMSA-Indonesia (the Indonesian
member association of the International Federation of Medical Students'
Associations). It targets **ages 17–25** with **"pre-activity training,
webinars, small working groups, talkshow, air campaigns, and challenge"**
([CASHEW program](../sources/cashew-indonesia-fast-fashion-program.md)).

## What it found before it did anything

Its own baseline survey is arguably the most useful figure in this wiki for
the "awareness" side of the challenge: **"more than 50% didn't know about
fast fashion and fashion waste"** despite recognizing environmental danger
in the abstract, and **"70% of respondents do not know how to overcome
it"** ([CASHEW program](../sources/cashew-indonesia-fast-fashion-program.md)).
That's a household-decision-making gap stated directly, not inferred.

## Scale

This is a small program, not a national or commercial one — participation
numbers per activity range from **73 participants** (an Instagram "Outfit of
the Day" challenge) to **364** (small working group discussions). Its
pre-activity training scored an **89.66 average post-test result**
([CASHEW program](../sources/cashew-indonesia-fast-fashion-program.md)).

## Relevance to our challenge

CASHEW is a concrete example of "smarter choices" being taught rather than
mandated or priced — closer to Orange County's K–12 curriculum response in
the [Los Angeles food-waste page](los-angeles.md) than to South Korea's
pay-by-weight system. It's small-scale evidence, not proof the approach
works at a population level: the post-test score measures short-term
knowledge gain, not a change in household buying or disposal behavior.
