# Programs and businesses already working on this

| Name | Region | What it does | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kloth Cares | Malaysia | Fabric collection nonprofit, founded 2016; collected 18,000+ kg in its first year against an estimated 2,000 tonnes/day of national textile waste | [source](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md) |
| Style Theory | Singapore | Clothing rental — rent an outfit for an event instead of buying it | [source](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md) |
| moreloop | Thailand | Connects factories' leftover "deadstock" fabric to buyers | [source](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md) |
| Fashion Revolution | 9 Southeast Asian countries | Global transparency/awareness campaign; regional coordinator says local awareness is "still in its infancy" | [source](../sources/sea-kloth-cares-circular-fashion.md) |
| Wear Again Lab | South Korea | Founder Juyeon Jung publicly pushing for regulated textile-waste handling in a system she says currently has none | [source](../sources/korea-fast-fashion-boom.md) |
| SB 707 Producer Responsibility Organization (pending) | California | Legally mandated EPR system for apparel producers — approved by CalRecycle, but first plan not due until 2030 | [source](../sources/la-sb707-textile-recovery.md) |
| Korea's food-waste RFID/fee system | South Korea (food, not textiles) | Not a fashion program, but the closest thing in this wiki to a *proven* mandatory household-waste system — included here as the model a textile program would have to match | [source](../sources/korea-food-waste-system.md) |

## The honest read

Of the seven rows above, only one — Korea's food waste system — has years of
track record showing it actually changed household behavior at scale (95%
recycling, per [that source](../sources/korea-food-waste-system.md)). Every
fashion-specific entry here is either small (Kloth Cares' first-year
collection is a rounding error against Malaysia's estimated daily waste), not
yet in effect (SB 707), or explicitly described by its own advocate as
unregulated and early-stage (Wear Again Lab, Fashion Revolution Malaysia).
That gap — a proven model in an adjacent waste stream, and no proven model
yet in fashion — is the opening a household-focused project would be filling.
