# Existing programs: Too Good To Go

Source: [Too Good To Go — consumer app for surplus
food](../sources/too-good-to-go-program.md)

Unlike the [Los Angeles](los-angeles.md) and [South Korea](south-korea.md)
pages, which are both government mandates acting on waste *after* it's
generated, Too Good To Go is a private, for-profit product that intervenes
**at the purchase decision** — before food becomes waste at all.

## What it is

Founded in **2016 in Copenhagen, Denmark**, and launched in the **US in
2021**. Participating grocery stores, bakeries, and restaurants package
unsold food into **"Surprise Bags"** at closing time; app users buy a bag
sight-unseen at **"one-third of the retail price,"** on average, and pick it
up in a set window
([Too Good To Go](../sources/too-good-to-go-program.md)).

## Scale

- **"In 2023, the app saved 121,686,720 meals from being wasted, and, as a
  result, prevented the emissions of 362,162 tons of carbon dioxide
  equivalents."**
- **"7.8 million users of the app worldwide"** (as of the same reporting).
- A separate, later figure states the app **"saved 157 million meals in
  2025."** This is a different year's total, not an update to the 2023
  figure, and the two shouldn't be added together
  ([Too Good To Go](../sources/too-good-to-go-program.md)).
- Business scale: **1,000 global staff**, operating in **27 US cities across
  15 states** per one source, with expansion into New Zealand and the US
  reported for 2025 in another
  ([Too Good To Go](../sources/too-good-to-go-program.md)).

## Relevance to our challenge

This is the clearest existing example of a "smarter choice about what to
buy" intervention at the household level — it doesn't require a government
mandate or new municipal infrastructure, and it directly targets [Target
12.3](sdg12-targets.md), the consumer-level food waste target. It's also the
one source in this wiki that comes from neither Los Angeles nor South Korea,
which is worth noting if the team wants a third-region or platform-based
angle instead of picking between the two.
