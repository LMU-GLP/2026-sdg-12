# How big is the fast fashion waste problem, globally

Source: [Global fast fashion waste stats](../sources/global-fast-fashion-waste-stats.md)

The fashion industry produces **"92 million tonnes of textiles waste...every
year,"** alongside **100 billion garments made annually** — described by the
Ellen MacArthur Foundation as **"one garbage truck of textiles wasted every
second."** That figure is projected to rise to **"up to 134 million tonnes a
year by the end of the decade"**
([Global fast fashion waste stats](../sources/global-fast-fashion-waste-stats.md)).

## Why "fast," specifically

Fast fashion brands now produce **"twice the amount of clothes today than
in 2000."** The industry's global emissions are projected to **increase by
50% by 2030**, and the sector as a whole already accounts for **nearly 10%
of global carbon emissions**
([Global fast fashion waste stats](../sources/global-fast-fashion-waste-stats.md)).

## Water and pollution

Producing **one kilogram of cotton takes 20,000 litres of water**; a single
T-shirt takes about **2,700 litres**. Textile dyeing and finishing accounts
for **3% of global CO2 emissions** and **20% of global water pollution**
([Global fast fashion waste stats](../sources/global-fast-fashion-waste-stats.md)).

## Microplastics

**Nearly 10% of microplastics** entering the ocean each year come from
textiles — about **half a million tons annually**, described as equivalent
to **50 billion bottles**
([Global fast fashion waste stats](../sources/global-fast-fashion-waste-stats.md)).

## The only country-level figure in this source: the US

This dataset's only country breakdown is American: **11.3 million tons of
textile waste** reach US landfills yearly (**81.5 pounds per person per
year**), and **2.6 million tonnes of returned clothes** ended up in
landfills in 2020 alone
([Global fast fashion waste stats](../sources/global-fast-fashion-waste-stats.md)).

## The Southeast Asia gap

This source has **no Southeast Asia-specific tonnage or per-capita figure**.
The [CASHEW program source](../sources/cashew-indonesia-fast-fashion-program.md)
separately states that Indonesia ranks second globally in textile waste
contribution, but that claim comes from a different source and isn't
corroborated by the global dataset here — see
[Southeast Asia: fast fashion](southeast-asia-fast-fashion.md) for how that's
handled.
