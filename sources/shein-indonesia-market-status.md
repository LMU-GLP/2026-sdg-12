# Shein's actual presence in Indonesia — a caveat for this project

- **Source:** Multiple, cross-referenced and partially conflicting —
  Business of Fashion, "Shein Shutters Indonesia Operations"; Rakyat News,
  "Government to Block Shein and Other Chinese Shopping Apps in Indonesia";
  ASEAN Briefing, "Indonesia's Social Media E-Commerce Ban"; general search
  results on Shein's 2018 Indonesia market entry.

- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures — and a real timeline conflict

Sources disagree on when, or how many times, Shein has left the Indonesian
market. Per CLAUDE.md's rule on disagreeing sources, both are kept here
rather than resolved into one story:

- One line of reporting: **Shein entered Indonesia in 2018 and exited,
  ceasing operations on July 29, 2021.**
- Indonesia's Ministry of Trade banned **social-media-based e-commerce
  transactions starting September 27, 2023** (the rule that also forced
  TikTok Shop out of direct social commerce), requiring platforms to hold a
  separate license, enforce a minimum price of **US$100 on certain overseas
  products**, and meet local quality standards.
- In **October 2024**, per Rakyat News, Indonesia's Ministry of
  Communication and Informatics (Kominfo) — having just moved to block
  Temu — **announced its intent to also block Shein**, with Minister Budi
  Arie Setiadi citing the need to **"protect local micro, small, and medium
  enterprises (MSMEs) from foreign competition."**
- A separate, later report (Business of Fashion, headline "Shein Shutters
  Indonesia Operations") indicates a notice on **Shein's Indonesian website
  as of August 2025** stating shipments to Indonesia had stopped. The full
  article could not be fetched (HTTP 403), so this is reported at the
  headline/summary level only, not verified against the full text.
- Context, for scale: **Chinese platforms including Shein and Temu account
  for roughly 50% of the e-commerce market in Indonesia, Thailand, and the
  Philippines as of 2024**, even as Indonesia's government has moved
  repeatedly to restrict them. Indonesia's own dominant e-commerce platform
  is **Shopee**, with **over 100 million users in Indonesia** specifically.

## Why this is a real problem for the pitch, not a minor detail

The project's mechanism depends on Shein having an active storefront/app in
Indonesia to embed a pop-up ad into. Based on what's sourced here, that's
not a safe assumption:

- Shein has a documented history of **entering and leaving** the Indonesian
  market, not a stable continuous presence.
- The Indonesian government has been **actively hostile** to platforms like
  Shein specifically (the 2023 social-commerce ban, the 2024 stated intent
  to block Shein by name), for reasons unrelated to sustainability —
  protecting domestic MSMEs from foreign price competition.
- The most recent signal found (Aug. 2025 shutdown notice) suggests Shein
  may not currently be shipping to Indonesia at all — which would mean there
  is no live pop-up to place an ad in, in this market, right now.

## Summary

Shein's presence in Indonesia is not the stable, ongoing operation the
pitch assumes: it entered in 2018, exited in 2021, has been targeted by
Indonesian government blocking efforts since at least 2024 (for
market-protection reasons, not environmental ones), and per one source may
have stopped shipping to Indonesia again as of August 2025. Any version of
this pitch that depends on "Shein's pop-up in Indonesia" needs to either
verify Shein currently operates there before building further, or change
which platform/market the pop-up mechanism targets.
