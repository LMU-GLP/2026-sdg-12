# Central Java Villages Take Fast Fashion to the Cleaners at Indonesia's Supreme Court

- **Source:** Mongabay
- **URL:** https://news.mongabay.com/2025/05/central-java-villages-take-fast-fashion-to-the-cleaners-at-indonesias-supreme-court/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **185 residents** of Central Java villages filed a class-action lawsuit
  against **PT Rayon Utama Makmur (RUM)**, a viscose/rayon fiber factory.
- Documented **air pollution across 7 subdistricts**: Nguter, Polokarto,
  Tawangsari, Bendosari, and Sukoharjo (Sukoharjo district), plus Selogiri
  and Wonogiri (Wonogiri district).
- The factory's wastewater pipe dumped pollution into the **Bengawan Solo
  River for approximately two months in 2022.**
- Indonesia's Supreme Court awarded **277.5 million rupiah (~$16,700)** for
  masks and medicine, and a further **222 million rupiah (~$13,400)** for
  medical bills. Ruling issued **December 16** (year of the ruling itself not
  stated in this article, distinct from the 2022 wastewater incident).
- For context: **10,000+ workers** at Sritex (a separate, nearby textile
  company) lost their jobs after that company's bankruptcy in March 2025 —
  cited in the article as regional context on the textile industry's
  instability, not connected to the RUM lawsuit itself.

## Environmental and health harms documented

- Carbon disulfide gas leaks affecting local air quality.
- Decline in local fish catch.
- Local bird population disappeared after the factory opened in 2017.

## Summary

185 Central Java residents won a Indonesian Supreme Court case against a
viscose/rayon fiber factory (PT RUM) after documented air and river pollution
across seven subdistricts caused health problems and drove away local
wildlife for years — a concrete, court-verified instance of harm from the raw
material supply chain that feeds fast fashion production, though the
plaintiffs themselves said money was never the point: they wanted the
pollution to stop.
