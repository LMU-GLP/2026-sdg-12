# The US Leads Global Secondhand Clothing Exports

- **Source:** Just Style (trade publication), citing UN Comtrade / industry
  trade data
- **URL:** https://www.just-style.com/news/in-data-us-leads-global-second-hand-clothing-exports-by-value/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- The **United States leads the world in secondhand clothing exports**, with
  a **17.9% share of the global market by value.**
- The US exports an estimated **700,000 metric tons of used clothing
  annually.**
- Runners-up: **China (15%), UK (7.5%), Germany (7%).**
- Globally, up to **4 million metric tonnes of used clothing** are traded
  each year.
- The article does not break down which specific countries receive US
  exports — it has no Southeast Asia– or Indonesia-specific destination data.
- Separately: if the estimated **1.6 billion unused garments sitting in
  American closets** entered the resale market, the article states this
  could prevent **255,747 metric tonnes of annual landfill waste** — a
  hypothetical, not a measured outcome.

## Note on scope

This source establishes the US as the largest exporter of used clothing by
volume and value — it does not name Indonesia or any Southeast Asian country
as a specific recipient. Pairing this figure with Indonesia's secondhand
import crackdown implies a connection (the US exports a lot; Indonesia
receives a lot of imports) but no source in this wiki traces a specific
shipment or percentage from the US to Indonesia specifically.

## Summary

The United States exports more used clothing than any other country — an
estimated 700,000 metric tons a year, 17.9% of the global market by value —
which is direct evidence that American household overconsumption produces a
large global secondhand-clothing waste stream, even though this source
doesn't say how much of that specific stream reaches Southeast Asia.
