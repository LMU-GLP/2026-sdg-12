# The Rise of Fast Fashion in South Korea, and How to Stop It

- **Source:** Earth.Org
- **URL:** https://earth.org/appearance-is-very-important-the-rise-of-fast-fashion-in-south-korea-and-how-to-stop-it/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- South Koreans spent **US$58 billion** on fashion products between March
  2024 and February 2025.
- South Korea discards an estimated **800,000 tonnes of clothing and
  textiles annually.**
- Only **12%** of that textile waste is recycled locally.
- **80%** of clothing-waste management is handled by the private sector, not
  government infrastructure.
- Used clothing exported from Korea goes primarily to **India, Malaysia, and
  Pakistan.**
- Korea's second-hand clothing market is expected to reach **US$30 billion**
  in value this year, having **doubled compared to 2021.**

## Direct quotes

> "From having a big car to owning many clothes, appearance is very important

> "The system we currently have is a problem... [it is] not regulated by the

## Note on contrast with Korea's food waste system

This is a sharp contrast with [Korea's food waste system](korea-food-waste-system.md),
which is government-run, mandatory, and metered by weight. For textiles,
Jung's account is that Korea has *no* equivalent government system: private
exporters, not regulation, currently absorb the country's used clothing.

## Summary

South Korea discards roughly 800,000 tonnes of clothing and textiles a year
and recycles only 12% of it locally, with 80% of the handling left to
unregulated private exporters rather than a government system — the opposite
of the mandatory, metered model Korea built for food waste, according to Wear
Again Lab founder Juyeon Jung, who says "we have to deal with our own waste."
