# Too Good To Go — consumer app for surplus food

- **Source:** NYC Food Policy Center, "Community Spotlight: Too Good To Go";
  cross-checked against RetailDetail EU, "Too Good To Go Saved 157 Million
  Meals in 2025," and New Food Magazine, "Too Good To Go Saves Over 100
  Million Meals From Being Wasted."
- **URL:** https://www.nycfoodpolicy.org/community-spotlight-too-good-to-go/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **Founded in 2016 in Copenhagen, Denmark; launched in the US in 2021.**
- Business model: a for-profit app with **"1,000 global staff members,"**
  operating in **27 US cities across 15 states** (as of the NYC Food Policy
  Center piece) and, per RetailDetail, expanding into **New Zealand and the
  United States in 2025** as part of a wider push beyond its original
  European markets.
- **"In 2023, the app saved 121,686,720 meals from being wasted, and, as a
  result, prevented the emissions of 362,162 tons of carbon dioxide
  equivalents."**
- **"7.8 million users of the app worldwide"** (figure given alongside the
  2023 meals-saved statistic).
- A later figure, from RetailDetail (2025 reporting), states **"Too Good To
  Go saved 157 million meals in 2025."** This is a different year's total
  than the 121.7 million figure above and the two should not be added or
  treated as the same measurement period.
- How it works: participating grocery stores, bakeries, and restaurants
  create **"Surprise Bags"** of unsold food at closing time; app users buy a
  bag, sight-unseen, at **"one-third of the retail price,"** on average, and
  pick it up in a set window.

## Direct quotes

> "purchase surplus food from local food-selling businesses"

> "the exact contents of the bags are not disclosed, but the app provides
> examples of what foods or dishes might be included."

## Note on conflicting figures

Different sources report different user counts and annual meal totals
(7.8 million users / 121.7 million meals in 2023 vs. 157 million meals in
2025) because they're measuring different years, not because the sources
disagree on the same fact. Both are kept here rather than reconciled into one
number.

## Summary

Too Good To Go is a for-profit app, founded in Copenhagen in 2016, that lets
households buy unsold "Surprise Bags" of food from local grocery stores,
bakeries, and restaurants at roughly a third of retail price shortly before
it would otherwise be thrown out. It reported saving 121.7 million meals and
preventing 362,162 tons of CO2-equivalent emissions in 2023 alone, and is one
of the few tools in this wiki that intervenes at the point of a household
purchase decision rather than through a government mandate.
