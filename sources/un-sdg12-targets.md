# SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production — Official Targets

- **Source:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs (Sustainable Development)
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal12
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures — the official targets

- **Target 12.3:** "By 2030, halve per capita global food waste at the retail and
  consumer levels and reduce food losses along production and supply chains,
  including post-harvest losses." (Indicator 12.3.1: food loss and waste indices)
- **Target 12.5:** "By 2030, substantially reduce waste generation through
  prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse." (Indicator 12.5.1: national
  recycling rate and tonnage of material recycled)
- **Target 12.4:** "By 2020, achieve the environmentally sound management of
  chemicals and all wastes throughout their life cycle... and significantly
  reduce their release to air, water and soil." (Indicator 12.4.2: hazardous
  waste generated per capita and proportion treated)
- **Target 12.8:** "By 2030, ensure that people everywhere have the relevant
  information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles in
  harmony with nature." (Indicator 12.8.1: extent to which sustainable
  development is mainstreamed in national education policy, curricula, teacher
  education, and student assessment)
- **Target 12.2:** "By 2030, achieve the sustainable management and efficient
  use of natural resources." (Indicators: material footprint and domestic
  material consumption, per capita and per GDP)
- **Target 12.6:** encourages companies, "especially large and transnational
  companies," to adopt sustainable practices and integrate sustainability
  information into their reporting cycle.
- **Target 12.1:** implement the 10-Year Framework of Programmes on
  Sustainable Consumption and Production Patterns.

## Note on scope

There is no textile- or fashion-specific target under Goal 12. Target 12.5
(waste generation, recycling, reuse) and Target 12.3 (food waste specifically)
are the two most directly relevant to a household-waste project; a fast-fashion
project sits under 12.5's general "waste generation" language and 12.2's
material-footprint language, not a dedicated clothing target.

## Summary

Goal 12's official framework has eight numbered targets plus three lettered
ones, and only one of them — 12.3 — names a specific waste stream (food) with
a specific number (halve it by 2030); the rest, including the target most
relevant to clothing, are phrased in terms of "waste generation" and
"resource efficiency" without a textile-specific figure. Any fast-fashion
target the team writes for its own pitch will be borrowed language, not a UN
number.
