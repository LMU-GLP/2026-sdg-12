# EU Exports of Used Textiles

- **Source:** European Environment Agency (EEA), "EU export of used textiles" (Textiles sectoral module, Circularity platform)
- **URL:** https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/circularity/sectoral-modules/textiles/eu-export-of-used-textiles
- **Read on:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- The export of used textiles from EU countries **"more than doubled to over 1.44 million tonnes in 2025"** compared with 2005.
- That works out to about **3.2 kg per person** across the EU in 2025.
- Export volumes have been **"relatively stable since 2015,"** aside from a dip in 2020 attributed to the Covid-19 pandemic.
- By destination in 2025: **48% of exports went to Asia**, **43% to Africa**. Pakistan and the United Arab Emirates received **"by far the most"** of any single countries.
- What happens to the clothing after export differs by region: in Africa it is described as **"mostly reused"**; in Asia, **"mostly recycled or re-exported."** In both regions, the EEA notes some portion **"end up in dumps or burned in the open."**

## Note on scope

This is EU-wide export data, not a Southeast-Asia-specific breakdown — the EEA groups destinations by continent ("Asia," "Africa"), so it doesn't say how many of the 1.44 million tonnes land specifically in Southeast Asian countries versus Pakistan, the UAE, or elsewhere in Asia. Pair with the UN Comtrade country-level import figures in [global secondhand clothing trade data](global-secondhand-clothing-trade-data.md), which does name Malaysia specifically.

## Summary

EU countries exported over 1.44 million tonnes of used textiles in 2025 — more than double the 2005 volume — with just under half going to Asia and most of the rest to Africa, but the EEA's own account of what happens next is uncertain: it says the clothing is "mostly recycled or re-exported" in Asia and "mostly reused" in Africa, while acknowledging some portion in both regions ends up dumped or burned in the open.
