# Fast Fashion and Textile Waste: Global Statistics

- **Source:** Earth.org, "10 Concerning Fast Fashion Waste Statistics"
  (aggregates figures credited to the Ellen MacArthur Foundation and other
  primary reports; original figures should be verified against the Ellen
  MacArthur Foundation directly if the team needs a primary citation)
- **URL:** https://earth.org/statistics-about-fast-fashion-waste/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **"92 million tonnes of textiles waste is produced every year,"** with
  **100 billion garments made annually.** Credited to the Ellen MacArthur
  Foundation, which frames this as **"one garbage truck of textiles wasted
  every second."**
- Projected to rise to **"up to 134 million tonnes a year by the end of the
  decade."**
- **"The apparel industry's global emissions will increase by 50% by
  2030."**
- Dyeing and finishing processes are responsible for **"3% of global CO2
  emissions"**; fast fashion overall accounts for **"nearly 10% of global
  carbon emissions."**
- Water: **"20,000 litres of water to produce one kilogram of cotton"**;
  about **"2,700 litres of water"** for one T-shirt. Dyeing/finishing is
  responsible for **"20% of global water pollution."**
- United States: **"11.3 million tons of textile waste...end up in
  landfills on a yearly basis"** — **"81.5 pounds per person per year."**
  Returned items alone: **"2.6 million tonnes of returned clothes ended up
  in landfills in 2020."**
- Microplastics: **"Nearly 10% of microplastics dispersed in the ocean each
  year come from textiles,"** amounting to **"half a million tons"**
  annually, described as equivalent to **"50 billion bottles."**
- Production growth: fast fashion brands produce **"twice the amount of
  clothes today than in 2000."**

## Note on this source

This is a secondary aggregator, not a primary report. It does not give a
Southeast Asia-specific breakdown — the country-level figures it has are for
the US only. For the Indonesia-specific angle in this wiki, see
[Indonesia secondhand clothing](../sources/indonesia-secondhand-clothing-crackdown.md)
and the [CASHEW program](../sources/cashew-indonesia-fast-fashion-program.md)
source, which separately states Indonesia ranks second globally in textile
waste contribution (uncorroborated by this source — flagged, not merged).

## Summary

The fashion industry produces about 92 million tonnes of textile waste a
year globally — projected to reach 134 million tonnes by 2030 — driven by
production that has doubled since 2000 and clothes worn far fewer times
before disposal. This global dataset has no Southeast Asia-specific number,
which is the gap the region-specific sources in this wiki fill.
