# UN SDG 12 Targets

The official framework for Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production) has eight numbered targets plus three lettered ones. Only one names a specific waste stream with a specific number; the rest are phrased generally. [Source](../sources/un-sdg12-targets.md)

## The targets most relevant to this project

- **Target 12.3:** "By 2030, halve per capita global food waste at the retail and consumer levels and reduce food losses along production and supply chains, including post-harvest losses." This is the only target with both a named waste stream (food) and a number (halve it).
- **Target 12.5:** "By 2030, substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse." This is the target a fast-fashion or textile-waste project sits under — general "waste generation" language, no clothing-specific figure.
- **Target 12.2:** "By 2030, achieve the sustainable management and efficient use of natural resources," measured by material footprint and domestic material consumption. Also relevant to fashion's raw-material use, again without a textile-specific figure.
- **Target 12.4:** environmentally sound management of chemicals and wastes, aimed at a 2020 deadline.
- **Target 12.8:** ensuring people have "the relevant information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles in harmony with nature" — the target closest to a household-behavior-change project.
- **Target 12.6:** encourages large and transnational companies to adopt sustainable practices and integrate sustainability reporting.
- **Target 12.1:** implement the 10-Year Framework of Programmes on Sustainable Consumption and Production Patterns.

## What this means for a fast-fashion project

There is **no textile- or fashion-specific target under Goal 12**. Any number this team uses to argue "fast fashion violates SDG 12 target X.X" is borrowed language, not a UN figure — the UN measures food waste (12.3) and general waste/material use (12.5, 12.2), not clothing specifically. [Source](../sources/un-sdg12-targets.md)

The UN itself has a body that exists specifically to fill this gap — see [International organizations' response](international-response.md) for the UN Alliance for Sustainable Fashion, launched in 2019 to coordinate UN action on fashion even though no Goal 12 target names it.
